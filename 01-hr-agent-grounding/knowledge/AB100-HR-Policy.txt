AB100 Employee HR Policy (short reference)
Last updated: 10 October 2026
Contact for exceptions: hr@ab100.example

1. Leave
- Casual leave: 12 days per calendar year.
- Sick leave: 8 days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.
- Earned leave: 15 days per calendar year. Unused earned leave can be carried forward up to 30 days.
- Leave request: apply in the HR portal at least 3 working days before the leave start date, except for sick leave.
- Leave approval: reporting manager approves casual and earned leave. HR records the balance.

2. Work from home (WFH)
- Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
- WFH must be approved by the reporting manager in the HR portal before the WFH day.
- Core hours on WFH days are 10:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time.
- WFH is not allowed on the day immediately before or after a company holiday unless HR approves an exception.

3. Holidays
Company holidays for 2026:
- 26 January — Republic Day
- 25 March — Holi
- 14 August — Independence Day (observed)
- 2 October — Gandhi Jayanti
- 20 October — Diwali
- 25 December — Christmas

The office is closed on the holidays listed above. These days do not count against leave balance.

4. What this file does not cover
This file does not define salary, bonus, notice period, resignation, parental leave, or visa policy.
For anything not written here, employees must contact HR. Do not assume a policy.
